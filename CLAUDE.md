**Objective**: I can apply my understanding of poetic language, structure, symbolism, imagery, voice, perspective and cultural reference to create an original work that communicates something meaningful about my own cultural perspective and understanding of the world.

* What have I learned from studying Oodgeroo’s poetry/writing about how creative choices can communicate culture and perspective?
* How can I use those choices to communicate something meaningful about my own world?
