# Project Brainstorm

## Requirements

* Communicate a meaningful **culture perspective**
* Demonstrate intentional use of at least FOUR **visual craft choices**
* Contain meaningful **symbolism or visual metaphor**
* Demonstrate **intentional composition**
* Require the audience to **interpret** rather than observe
* Include a **title**

## Aspects
* **Visual Craft Project Idea**
    * Title: "Life Is My Teacher"
    * An ASCII animation
    * Animation opens up in a separate, perfectly sized, rightly zoomed command prompt window
    * The chinese phrase "三人行" bounces around the box like the "DVD" animation
        * The phrase is in a filled box and hollowed out to make it

* **Culture perspective**
    * Chinese background
    * Interests in technology and computer science

* **Symbolism**
    * The title "Life Is My Teacher" combines the proverb with the purpose of this presentation: I learn from life
    * The Chinese phrase "三人行" (sān rén xíng) which comes from "三人行，必有我师" (when three walk together, there must be my teacher) in the Analects of Confucius
        * The proverb calls for humility, self-reflection, and the idea that every human interaction is an opportunity for personal moral growth
    * Like the DVD animation, I hope I hit a corner, that I will ultimately reaach a certian destination or accomplish something amazing
        * The DVD animation represents my life, bouncing everywhere, discovering myself, and ending up somewhere
    * ASCII animation to show my interest in computer science
    
* **Four visual craft choices**
    * DVD animation
        * Chinese proverb instead of DVD
    * Border of the screen is constructed using the word "LIFE"
    * The chinese characters are constructed using the word "ME"
    * Chose to make an ASCII animation to convey my love for computer science
